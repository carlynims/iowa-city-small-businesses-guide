Map of Small Businesses in Iowa City

By Dev Raman and Marlene Okafor

Wondering where the independent shops, cafés, and studios are? This map shows the Iowa City small businesses we feature, grouped by neighborhood so you can see at a glance which ones sit close together and which are worth a short trip across town.

The Map

This is a simplified diagram, not drawn to scale. It shows how the neighborhoods relate to one another, not exact street layouts or walking distances.

How the Neighborhoods Connect

Downtown sits at the center of the map and holds the largest cluster of Iowa City small businesses, so it is the easiest place to start. Everything in the downtown group is close enough to visit on one walk along the Pedestrian Mall.

The Northside, Near Southside, and Riverfront Crossings each form their own small hubs. They are a short walk, bike ride, or bus ride from downtown, which makes them good second stops once you have explored the center.

Using the Map to Plan Your Day

Walk Downtown

Pins 1 through 8 fit into a relaxed afternoon on foot. Start with coffee and end with records.

Bike Between Areas

The neighborhoods are close enough that a bike is often the quickest way to hop from one cluster to the next.

Pair Your Stops

Combine a downtown morning with a Northside bakery run, or pair a hardware errand with fabric shopping on the Near Southside.
