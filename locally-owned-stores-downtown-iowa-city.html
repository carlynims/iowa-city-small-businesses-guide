Locally-Owned Stores Downtown Iowa City: A Walking Guide

By Marlene Okafor

Downtown Iowa City is one of the best places in eastern Iowa to spend an afternoon wandering through locally-owned stores. Within a few blocks of the Pedestrian Mall you can buy a used novel, a vinyl record, a vintage jacket, and a fern, with a coffee in your hand the whole time. Here is our guide to the shops worth your steps.

Books, Paper, and Music

Hollow Oak Books

Used and new books, with a strong poetry shelf and a corner of regional history. Staff recommendation slips are tucked into favorites, and the owner keeps a reading chair by the front window for anyone who wants to sit.

Paper Wren Stationery

Letterpress cards, planners, fountain pens, and wrapping paper printed by Midwest artists. It is a lovely stop if you still like writing thank-you notes by hand.

Second Chorus Records

A compact record shop with crates of jazz, folk, indie, and classic rock. The owner will happily play anything before you buy it, and there is a dollar bin in the back for the curious.

Clothing and Vintage

Fieldhouse Threads

Locally printed apparel, from everyday T-shirts to game-day sweatshirts. Everything is designed by Iowa artists and printed in a studio just outside town.

Juniper & Ash Vintage

Racks of carefully chosen vintage coats, denim, and knitwear, priced fairly for students. The owner sources most of the stock from estate sales across the state.

Home, Gifts, and Treats

Marigold & Moss

A bright plant and gift shop with hardy houseplants that survive dorm windows and drafty apartments. Staff write care instructions on every pot.

Sugar Maple Candy Counter

An old-fashioned candy counter with hand-pulled taffy, chocolate-dipped pretzels, and seasonal fudge. It is a reliable stop for last-minute gifts.

Cobblestone Coffee Roasters

A roaster that sells beans by the pound and pours drinks all day. It makes a good start or finish to your walk, and the back room has outlets for anyone who needs to work for an hour.

Planning Your Walk

  • Allow about three hours if you want to browse every shop without rushing.
  • Start at the Pedestrian Mall and fan out to the nearby streets, since the stores sit close together.
  • Check hours before you go. Many independent shops open later in the morning and close earlier on Sundays than chain stores do.
  • Parking: parking ramps and metered street spots are available around downtown, and the area is easy to explore on foot once you arrive.
  • Visit midweek for quieter aisles and more time to chat with the owners.
Visiting on the biggest shopping day of the year? Our Small Business Saturday plan turns this list into a timed route. For shops beyond downtown, see our Iowa City small business guide.

Why Shop at Locally-Owned Stores?

A locally-owned store reflects the person behind the counter. The owner picks the stock, knows the regulars, and decides what the shop stands for. When you choose these stores, you help keep downtown varied, interesting, and different from every other town on the map.
