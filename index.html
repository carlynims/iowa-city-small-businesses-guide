Discover the Small Businesses That Make Iowa City Feel Like Home

Linden & Larch is a friendly, no-nonsense guide to the independent bookstores, coffee roasters, bakeries, record shops, and makers that give Iowa City its character. Whether you are a longtime resident, a first-year student, or a visitor in town for a weekend, we will help you find places worth your time and your dollars.

Featured This Season

Hollow Oak Books

A cozy downtown shop stacked floor to ceiling with used and new titles. The staff pencil short recommendation notes on slips of paper tucked into their favorite books, and regulars treat those notes like treasure maps.

Pennycress Bakery

A Northside bakery known for cardamom morning buns and sourdough that sells out by noon on weekends. Arrive early, bring cash for the tip jar, and grab a seat by the window.

Riverbend Hardware

A family-run hardware store where someone will actually walk you to the right aisle. If you rent your first apartment in town, this is where to buy the shelf brackets and the advice.

Why We Built This Guide

Linden & Larch was started by editor Marlene Okafor, a former downtown shop manager, and writer Dev Raman, who spent four years in Iowa City as a student and never found one clear place to learn what the town's independent businesses had to offer. So they made it.

We follow two simple rules. First, we only feature businesses that are independently owned and operated in the Iowa City area. Second, no business can pay for a better write-up. If we recommend a place, it is because we would send our own friends there.

New here? Begin with our guide to Iowa City small businesses for the big picture, then use the downtown walking guide when you are ready to go shopping on foot.
